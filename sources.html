Scientific Sources & Public Health Evidence

Public health initiatives are only as trustworthy as the evidence behind them. Below are the municipal, federal, global, and community sources informing our disease prevention guidelines and validating Aid for AIDS of Nevada's work.

Local Las Vegas Health Authority Clark County, NV • 2024-2026

Southern Nevada Health District (SNHD)

Infectious Disease Surveillance, Sexual Health Clinic & Community Nursing Programs

SNHD is the accredited municipal public health agency for Clark County, NV. Their epidemiological surveillance reports track incidence rates of HIV, Syphilis, Hepatitis, respiratory syncytial virus (RSV), and Influenza in Southern Nevada. They establish local PrEP/PEP clinic standards and coordinate immunization services with community non-profits like AFAN.

Key takeaway utilized: Establishes Clark County STI testing frequency recommendations, emergency PEP clinical protocols, and local free clinic accessibility.

Featured Nevada Nonprofit Lead Founded 1984 • Las Vegas, NV

Aid for AIDS of Nevada (AFAN)

Community HIV Care, Prevention Education, Ryan White Coordination & Client Support

Nevada's oldest and largest AIDS service organization. AFAN provides direct client case management, free testing navigation, nutritional pantry support, medical transportation, and emergency housing assistance. Serving over 1,200 individuals and families across Clark County annually, AFAN serves as the frontline community safety net against disease transmission and isolation.

Key takeaway utilized: Provides direct program metrics, client service models (housing, food pantry, transit vouchers), Las Vegas headquarters details (1830 E. Sahara Ave.), and community event organizing (AIDS Walk Las Vegas).

Federal Public Health Agency Atlanta, GA • 2024

Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)

HIV PrEP & PEP Clinical Guidelines, Core Infection Prevention and Control Practices (IPC)

The national public health institute of the United States. Author of clinical provider guidance on Pre-Exposure Prophylaxis (PrEP), Post-Exposure Prophylaxis (PEP) within 72 hours, hand hygiene metrics (20-second minimum wash with friction), mask filtration benchmarks (N95/KN95), and food safety temperature standards.

Key takeaway utilized: Verified efficacy rates of PrEP (~99% reduction via sexual intercourse; 74% via IDU), the critical 72-hour window for PEP, and universal droplet/airborne precautions.

International Health Authority Geneva, Switzerland • 2024

World Health Organization (WHO)

Global Health Sector Strategies on HIV, Viral Hepatitis and STIs & IPC Standards

Specialized agency of the United Nations responsible for international public health. Defines global harm reduction standards, sterile syringe programming, airborne versus contact pathogen transmission classification, and universal healthcare coverage guidelines for antiretroviral therapies.

Key takeaway utilized: Global infection prevention and control (IPC) frameworks and harm reduction syringe exchange protocols.

Biomedical Research Authority Bethesda, MD • 2023-2025

National Institutes of Health (NIH / NIAID)

PARTNER 1, PARTNER 2 & HPTN 052 Landmark Clinical Trials (Undetectable = Untransmittable)

National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases led and funded the landmark clinical trials verifying the scientific basis for U=U. By monitoring serodiscordant couples over tens of thousands of condomless acts, these studies proved zero documented cases of sexual HIV transmission when viral load is suppressed below 200 copies/mL.

Key takeaway utilized: Validates the biological and epidemiological certainty of U=U, dismantling fear and demonstrating the power of consistent medication adherence.

State Government Strategic Plan Carson City & Las Vegas • 2024

Nevada Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS)

End HIV Nevada Strategic Plan & Fast-Track Cities Southern Nevada Consortium

State-level initiative coordinating Nevada's 95-95-95 targets (95% diagnosed, 95% on treatment, 95% virally suppressed). Manages federal Ryan White Part B allocations, state medication assistance programs, and public-private contracts with Aid for AIDS of Nevada.

Key takeaway utilized: Outlines state funding mechanisms and highlights AFAN as a key community subcontractor delivering Ryan White services in Clark County.
