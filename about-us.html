About Our Initiative & AFAN Las Vegas

We are an educational public health coalition united by a single vision: empowering the people of Southern Nevada with life-saving disease prevention knowledge while rallying broad community support for Aid for AIDS of Nevada (AFAN).

The Historic Foundation

Aid for AIDS of Nevada: 40+ Years of Compassion

Est. 1984 • Las Vegas, NV

In 1984, when fear, stigma, and medical uncertainty surrounded the emergence of HIV and AIDS in the United States, a dedicated cohort of compassionate volunteers in Las Vegas took action. They formed Aid for AIDS of Nevada (AFAN), determined to ensure that no neighbor in Southern Nevada would face this diagnosis in isolation or neglect.

Today, AFAN stands proud as Nevada’s oldest and largest AIDS service organization. Operating out of their main headquarters at 1830 E. Sahara Ave., Suite 210, Las Vegas, NV 89104, AFAN delivers holistic, barrier-free assistance to more than 1,200 individuals, children, and families each year throughout Clark County.

While biomedical science has revolutionized HIV from a terminal illness into a manageable chronic condition with U=U (Undetectable = Untransmittable), social determinants of health—such as housing insecurity, malnutrition, and systemic stigma—remain critical hurdles. AFAN addresses the complete human being, ensuring medical care is backed by food, shelter, and emotional dignity.

Prevention & Testing

Fast, free, confidential HIV screening and navigation to PrEP and PEP medications to stop transmission before it occurs.

Housing & Survival Support

Rental grants, utility relief, and emergency food pantry supplies that prevent client homelessness and immune decline.

Dignity & Anti-Stigma

Comprehensive community outreach, support groups, and events that replace prejudice and fear with love and empowerment.

Education as the First Line of Defense

Many Nevadans are unaware that taking PrEP daily almost completely eliminates HIV risk, or that PEP can reverse exposure within 72 hours. Misconceptions about how respiratory viruses, enteric pathogens, and bloodborne infections spread still persist. Our platform makes clinical evidence plain, accessible, and actionable.
