Why I Built This Website

Living in Nevada, I have seen firsthand how critical accessible, straightforward healthcare education really is. When infectious disease strikes—whether it is seasonal influenza, respiratory viruses like COVID-19, or chronic conditions like HIV and viral hepatitis—the greatest barrier to safety is rarely a lack of medicine. It is a lack of accessible information and the fear of stigma.

Too many people in Southern Nevada don't know that daily or injectable PrEP can prevent HIV with ∼99% effectiveness. Too many people are unaware that emergency PEP can protect them within a 72-hour window. And tragically, outdated myths still lead people to feel shame about getting tested or asking for help.

Why Aid for AIDS of Nevada (AFAN) Has My Full Heart

When looking for an organization in Nevada doing the actual, tireless frontline work, no organization compares to Aid for AIDS of Nevada (AFAN). Founded in Las Vegas in 1984, AFAN has spent over four decades serving as the safety net for our most vulnerable neighbors.

AFAN doesn't just talk about prevention—they deliver it directly at 1830 E. Sahara Ave. They provide bilingual medical case managers, stock a full nutrition pantry for clients, pay emergency utility bills so families don't lose power in the blistering Vegas summer, and offer taxi and bus vouchers so nobody misses a clinic visit.

My Core Health Guiding Principles:

01. Science Over Stigma

U=U is a verified medical truth. People with undetectable HIV viral loads do not transmit the virus. Stigma has zero scientific basis.

02. Accessible Prevention

Handwashing, high-grade filtration, clean needles, and barrier protections are fundamental public health rights for every person.

03. Support Local Heroes

Local non-profits like AFAN Las Vegas are the heartbeat of community survival. Supporting them protects everyone.
