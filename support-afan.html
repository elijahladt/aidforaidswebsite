How You Can Support AFAN Las Vegas

For over 40 years, Aid for AIDS of Nevada (AFAN) has stood as Southern Nevada's guardian against HIV transmission, poverty, and isolation. Here is how your generosity and time change lives in Clark County right now.

1. Financial Giving & Monthly Support

Monetary gifts allow AFAN to respond dynamically to urgent client needs. Your tax-deductible donation directly pays for life-saving medication co-pays, rent to prevent eviction, utility restorations, and fresh nutritious food.

  • $25: Provides round-trip transit bus passes for 5 medical appointments.
  • $50: Feeds an immunocompromised individual with pantry groceries for a week.
  • $150+: Emergency utility stipend keeping power on in extreme Las Vegas heat.
Donate Securely at afanlv.org →

2. AIDS Walk Las Vegas

AFAN's signature annual event, AIDS Walk Las Vegas, gathers thousands of advocates, community partners, celebrities, and families. Walk as an individual or create a corporate or school team to raise funds and smash HIV stigma.

Impact: Over three decades of solidarity in Clark County.

Community: Live entertainment, wellness vendors, free rapid testing, family & pet friendly.

Register or Sponsor a Walk Team →

3. Food Pantry & In-Kind Donations

Good nutrition is essential for immune strength when fighting disease. AFAN operates a community food pantry at their Sahara office. You can organize a food drive or drop off items directly.

  • • Canned proteins (tuna, chicken, salmon, peanut butter)
  • • Whole grains (brown rice, oats, whole wheat pasta)
  • • Unopened hygiene essentials (toilet paper, soap, shampoo, oral care)
  • • Grocery store gift cards (Smith's, Albertsons, Walmart)
Call (702) 382-2326 for Drop-Off Hours

4. Volunteer Your Time

Volunteers are the lifeblood of AFAN, just as they were back in 1984. Help with pantry packing, event logistics at the Black & White Party, clerical support, or educational community tabling across Las Vegas.

Opportunities: Flexible weekend, daytime, and event-based shifts.

Who can join: Students, corporate groups, retirees, passionate community members.

Sign Up to Volunteer at afanlv.org →
